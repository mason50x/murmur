## 1.0.1 — 2026-10-11

First public release.

- Push-to-talk dictation: hold the right Option key, talk, let go. Double-tap for hands-free.
- Transcription on the Neural Engine with NVIDIA Parakeet. Audio never leaves your Mac.
- Picks a speech model for your Mac: Parakeet Unified on 16 GB+, Phonon-2 on 8 GB. Switch in Settings.
- Pastes into the focused field and restores your clipboard; shows a copy card when nothing can take the text.
- Fits casing and spacing to the text around your cursor, and lays out lists and paragraphs from how you spoke.
- A setup guide for the model, microphone and Accessibility access, with a practice run.
- Settings for the microphone, sounds, login item, model, history and updates.
- Automatic updates, signed and verified with Sparkle.
